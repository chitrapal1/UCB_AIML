**Findings : ** 
About Bar coupons:
Drivers who accept bar coupons are typically younger, socially active, and price‑sensitive individuals. 
They tend to visit bars more than once a month, are less likely to have kids as passengers, and are not widowed. 
They also show higher responsiveness when their lifestyle already includes frequent inexpensive dining and when their income is below $50K.
